- six successful timed calls: search, entity context, schema, upstream lineage, downstream lineage and generating queries
- current active incidents and **0 writes during proof**

that the visual was backed by DataHub MCP responses, not hard-coded labels.

and memory layer so agents can inspect evidence before acting.”

## 0:45–1:55 — Seven-case RL mission theatre

Select **Biomarker Completeness Crisis** and ask:

> Which cancer progression datasets are trustworthy, and what downstream models would be affected by a completeness issue?

Click at least three different condition worlds and point to the different exact
URN, owner, contract and six-call receipt for each. Then point to the live MCP search/entity/schema/lineage/query calls and the exact
DataHub URN. Then point to the RL state, ranked action and reward: the controller
blocks the ML consumer before repair. Approve once; DataHub resolves the real
incident and the app verifies zero active incidents.

Use **Verified Replay** to show the complete trace without repeating the write.
Use Proof Galaxy to click the other six worlds. State clearly that all seven have
dedicated live DataHub products and a guarded condition incident path, while the
biological telemetry and RL consequences are research simulations.

Keep the 3D twin in view while the mission runs. Narrate the synchronized scene reactions: catalog sweep → amber quality warning → lineage pulse → lesion focus → governance lock. This is the visual proof that DataHub context is driving application state.

Start on **Live Mission**, matching the primary judge view. Select a second cohort from the left rail to prove the application is not a LUAD-only mockup, then return to LUAD and launch the incident. Click two different agent nodes while the mission runs to expose their tools and returned context.
